02 · About

Curious about what happens between design and reality.

I study elastic and mechanical metamaterials , with a particular interest in how their geometry and material behaviour govern wave propagation and mechanical response.

My PhD at the University of Groningen focuses on the experimental characterisation of wave dynamics in additively manufactured polymer metamaterials. The broader goal is to make programmable, adaptive and multifunctional structures more predictable and useful.

Experimental

LDV · DIC · UToFD · DMA · Rheology · TGA · UTM

Manufacturing
